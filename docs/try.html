Try it

A house of your own, in a minute.

Every visitor gets a private copy of the demo family: Maya, who is allergic to peanuts, Bruno the dog, and Priya, who is babysitting tonight. Nothing you do reaches anyone else's house. The demo runs on a free host: the first visit after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to wake it.

Four ways to try it

01 · THE ECHO SHOW

The simulator, as Alexa+

Press Watch the highlights (2½ minutes, hands-free) or the whole evening (about five), or talk to it. Scan the QR code to be Mom on your own phone: her questions reach you, and the evening waits for your answer.

Open the live demo →

02 · TODAY'S ALEXA

An Alexa-hosted skill

The same add-on on a real Echo, free to host: "Alexa, open while you are out." Five minutes to set up in your own developer console, in English or Hindi.

Set it up →

03 · ANY MCP HOST

Claude, VS Code, the Inspector

One page gives you your house's MCP address and how to add it, plus the parent page for the same house. An agent hosting it should follow the add-on's Agent Skill.

Get your address →

04 · EVERY SCREEN

All 26 cards, every device

Real tool results from one scripted evening, rendered by the add-on's own card on an Echo Show 5, 8, 15 and 21, an Echo Hub and a phone, in dark and light.

See the gallery →
