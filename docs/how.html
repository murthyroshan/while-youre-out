How it works

One add-on, three ways in.

While You're Out is an MCP server: the open protocol Alexa+ add-ons are built on. Alexa+ calls its tools and shows its cards on the Echo Show; the parents' and the sitter's phones talk to the same house.

What it uses, and why

Each one carries part of the evening; none is there for its own sake.

FeatureWhere it shows up
MCP Apps cardEvery answer on the Echo Show: the Tonight screen, the emergency guide, the doorbell photo, the goodnight message. One card, sized by the device class, in light and dark.
Tasks"Ask Mom if she can stay up": the question waits for a person, and the answer arrives when she taps it on her lock screen.
Form elicitation"We're heading out" with no name: the Echo Show asks who is babysitting, their language and rate.
URL elicitation"Pay Priya": the payment opens on the parent's own phone, never on the shared screen.

Output schemas, annotations, status textEvery tool says what it returns, whether it changes anything, and what to show while it works.
OAuth 2.1 account linkingA household links once (PKCE, refresh rotation, protected-resource metadata); the demo needs no sign-in.

OPEN SOURCE

The simulator is yours too

Alexa+ isn't open to everyone yet, so I built a stand-in for the host: an Echo Show simulator that talks to any MCP add-on the Alexa+ way, with screens for every Echo device, a card audit and a readiness score. It's a project of its own, MIT licensed.
